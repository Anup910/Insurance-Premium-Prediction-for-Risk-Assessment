# Insurance-Premium-Prediction-for-Risk-Assessment
Problem Statement:
InsuraTech Solutions aims to optimize its insurance underwriting process by accurately predicting insurance premiums for customers based on their demographic and personal information. Currently, the company struggles with generalized premium models that either overcharge or undercharge customers, leading to customer dissatisfaction and potential financial risks. To address this, InsuraTech Solutions requires a data-driven solution to analyze customer data and generate precise predictions for premiums, ensuring fair pricing and optimized risk management.

Project Overview:
The project is designed to develop a machine learning pipeline for predicting insurance premiums. Using a dataset comprising customer demographics, health metrics, and lifestyle factors, the project demonstrates the preprocessing steps, exploratory data analysis (EDA), and model building required to achieve accurate predictions. The solution caters to InsuraTech’s objectives of improving premium calculation accuracy and operational efficiency.

Features from the dataset:

PolicyID: A unique identifier for each insurance policy.
Exposure: The duration of time a policy was active and exposed to risk.
ClaimNb: The number of claims made during the exposure period.
ClaimAmount: The total cost of claims during the exposure period.
ClaimFreq: Frequency of claims (possibly derived as ClaimNb/Exposure).
ClaimSev: Severity of claims, often representing average claim amount per claim.
DriverAge: The age of the driver associated with the policy.
CarAge: The age of the insured vehicle.
Power: The power or engine capacity of the vehicle.
Brand: The manufacturer or brand of the vehicle.
Gas: The type of fuel used by the vehicle (e.g., petrol, diesel).
Region: The geographical region where the policyholder resides.
Density: Population density of the policyholder's region
